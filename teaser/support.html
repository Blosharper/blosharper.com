Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data in the game: Settings › Data Management › Wipe All Data / Reset Profile. This deletes your progress, stats and settings on the device, and if you are signed in to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, it also overwrites your cloud save with an empty one. This cannot be undone.
